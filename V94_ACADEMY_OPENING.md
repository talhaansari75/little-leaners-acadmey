# V94 — Little Learners Academy Opening Experience

## First impression
- Replaced the old word-search splash with a full-screen Little Learners Academy opening scene.
- Uses the bundled offline Academy artwork as the visual world, with layered lighting, depth, sparkles and a gentle Ken Burns motion.
- Presents the Academy brand, mascot, Learn • Play • Grow message, three class pathways, and a clear Enter the Academy CTA.
- Tapping anywhere enters the app immediately; otherwise the opening advances automatically after 4.2 seconds.
- Audio is unlocked and existing music preferences are respected when entering.
- Reduced-motion users receive a non-animated version.
- No network request is required for the opening artwork.
