# V84 — Classroom Curriculum Expansion

## Three class pathways
- Nursery: early language, counting, colors/shapes, creative play and practical-life routines.
- KG: phonics, CVC word building, number sense, early operations, patterns, reading and school-readiness skills.

## Curriculum UX
- Child can switch between Nursery, KG and KG.
- Subject shelves: Language, Math, Creative and Life.
- Each skill can be practiced and marked complete.
- Class/subject progress is stored locally for offline use.

## Montessori expansion
- Practical Life: pouring, scooping, folding, dressing fasteners, lacing, hygiene, table setting and organization.
- Sensorial: size/length grading, color, shape, texture, sound, weight and patterns.
- Language: sound games, sandpaper-style tracing, object naming, sound sorting, word building and reading.
- Mathematics: number rods, sandpaper numbers, quantity, spindle-box counting, zero, ten frames, tens/ones and concrete addition.
- Culture & Nature: land/water, continents, habitats, plants, weather, community, maps and observation.

All activities are presented as short digital learning simulations; they do not claim to replace hands-on Montessori materials or teacher guidance.
