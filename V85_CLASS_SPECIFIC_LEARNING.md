# V85 — Class-Specific Learning

## Exactly three pathways
- Nursery
- KG
- Montessori

Nursery, KG and Montessori have separate curriculum shelves, progress keys, offline worksheets and offline audio lesson folders.

## Nursery
Early language, counting 1–10, colors/shapes, creative play, listening, rhymes and practical routines.

## KG
Phonics, CVC word building, reading, numbers to 100, early addition/subtraction, patterns, science, writing and school readiness.

## Montessori
Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics and Culture & Nature remain a separate Montessori pathway.

- `public/offline/preschool/audio/kg/`
- `public/offline/preschool/audio/montessori/`
- `public/offline/preschool/worksheets/nursery/`
- `public/offline/preschool/worksheets/kg/`
- `public/offline/preschool/worksheets/montessori/`

The bundled audio is synthesized instructional speech generated for the app; it is not a recording of a human teacher. Core files are local and do not require a network connection.
