# V6 Preschool Expansion

Adds a larger offline-first preschool layer on top of V5:
- Daily Adventure learning map with six repeatable quests and device-local progress.
- Smart Teacher with adaptive topic suggestion based on age, XP and completed activities.
- Art Studio with color palette, shapes/stickers, clear and local persistence.
- Story Universe expanded from 3 to 20 short stories with 20 bundled SVG illustrations.
- New Adventure, Teacher and Art navigation areas.
- Existing V5 Music World, Mini Games and My Little World remain available.

Core learning content is offline-friendly. Speech synthesis remains device/browser dependent.
